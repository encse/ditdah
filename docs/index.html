A companion for learning CW

Listen along.
Get a little help.

DitDah is a terminal-based CW companion written in Go, built for beginner operators who are starting to make real QSOs but still miss a few characters. It writes along with the signal, then helps you put the contact in your log and optionally sync it to QRZ.com.

A quiet evening on the bands

From signal to text.
Without breaking your flow.

Find a signal on your radio, tune it carefully, and narrow the filter until the beeps are easy to hear. DitDah listens to the same audio and gives you a readable second opinion while you copy it yourself.

02

Logbook + QSO editor

Log the contact when you make it.

Move the callsign straight into a new QSO, fill in the report and anything else you want to remember, then save it locally. If you use QRZ.com, completed contacts can be synchronized from the same logbook.

  • Prefilled QSO from the decoded callsign
  • Simple local logbook with search
  • Optional QRZ.com lookup and synchronization
01

Live Morse decoder

Start with a signal

DitDah uses a neural network trained to decode Morse between 10 and 40 WPM. It is reasonably tolerant of background noise, but a well-tuned, filtered signal helps the decoder just as much as it helps your ears.

  • Use your transciever's USB audio interface or a microphone when the source is loud and clear enough
  • Route audio from an SDR or WebSDR through a virtual input such as BlackHole on macOS
  • Keep callsigns and QRZ details beside the decoded text

Designed for relaxed operating

A helper, not an autopilot.

DitDah is meant to sit beside you while your Morse ear develops. Listen first, glance at the text when you need it, and take your time with the contact.

Good fit

One clear signal and an unhurried QSO.

Calling CQ, answering a station, casual conversations, and listening practice at a comfortable speed.

Not for

Pileups, contests, or separating several stations.

DitDah does not replace careful tuning, contest software, or your own judgment about what was actually sent.
